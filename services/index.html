Services

Training, AI delivery, bioinformatics support, and GA4/GTM implementation for teams that need practical execution, not slideware.

I help companies, universities, and product teams turn data and analytics into operational capability. My work combines technical delivery, strategic guidance, and hands-on training, with a focus on four areas:

  • Training for teams, business schools, and executive education
  • Machine Learning and AI consulting from use case definition to production
  • Bioinformatics support for applied research and health-data workflows
  • GA4 and GTM implementation for measurement, tracking, and digital marketing operations

Across all four areas, the typical objective is the same: build a working system, upskill the team, and leave behind a setup that is understandable, measurable, and maintainable.

How I usually work#

  • Discovery session to clarify business goals, data availability, constraints, and deadlines
  • Audit of the current stack, tracking, workflows, or models
  • Delivery plan with concrete milestones, dependencies, and success criteria
  • Hands-on execution, documentation, and stakeholder training

Engagement formats#

  • Advisory and audits
  • Project-based delivery
  • Fractional specialist support
  • In-company training and workshops
  • Guest lecturing for universities and business schools
